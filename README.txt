HAPPY 54th BIRTHDAY, DAD!
=========================

How to open your birthday website:

  1. If this came as a .zip file, unzip it first
     (right-click the zip -> "Extract All..." -> Extract).
     It won't work if you open it from inside the zip.

  2. Open the "dad-birthday" folder.

  3. Double-click index.html to open.

That's it. No internet needed.

Tips:
  - Scroll slowly and enjoy.
  - Press "Replay" on the first screen to watch the fireworks again.
  - Use the buttons at the top to visit "Around the World", "Silly Dad"
    "My Dad is Cooler Than Yours" and "Food + Family".
  - Works best in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.
